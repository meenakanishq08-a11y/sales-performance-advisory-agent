# SALES PERFORMANCE AND GOVERNANCE PLAYBOOK

## SECTION 1: DISCOUNT POLICY AND PRICING GOVERNANCE
- **Standard Discount Authority**: Account executives and sales representatives are authorized to offer a standard commercial discount of up to 10% on all standard hardware and software bundles (including Tab-X and Tab-Y).
- **Escalation & Approval Threshold**: Any discount exceeding 10% is strictly prohibited without prior written authorization from the Regional Sales Director or Commercial Finance Head.
- **Promise Protocol**: Representatives must NEVER promise, commit to, or quote unapproved discounts exceeding 10% to clients or prospects before securing official management approval.
- **Audit Compliance**: All approved exception discounts must be logged in the deal governance ledger with signed approval artifacts.

## SECTION 2: LOW REVENUE REMEDIATION POLICY
- **Trigger**: Triggered when monthly target achievement is below 90% or MoM revenue growth is negative.
- **Recommended Actions**:
  1. Re-evaluate pipeline velocity and qualify active deals scheduled for the current quarter.
  2. Implement targeted product bundling (e.g. Tab-X with extended warranty or support package).
  3. Offer value-add incentives rather than steep price cuts.
  4. Conduct bi-weekly executive check-ins with top 20% tier-1 accounts in the region.

## SECTION 3: LOW MARGIN REMEDIATION POLICY
- **Trigger**: Triggered when gross margin percentage drops below 15% or unit realization approaches unit cost.
- **Policy Guidelines**:
  1. Halt discretionary discounting on low-margin SKUs.
  2. For Tab-X (unit cost 2400) and Tab-Y (unit cost 1800), prioritize high-margin accessory attachments and software subscriptions.
  3. Review shipping and distribution expenses with regional logistics partners to lower landed costs.
  4. Require minimum order quantities (MOQs) for custom pricing concessions.

## SECTION 4: HIGH RETURNS MITIGATION POLICY
- **Trigger**: Triggered when product return rate exceeds 5% of shipped units.
- **Mitigation Protocols**:
  1. Quarantine problematic batches and initiate Quality Assurance inspection with manufacturing.
  2. Provide immediate customer onboarding and technical integration training for affected accounts.
  3. Investigate if return surge is tied to defective hardware revisions or improper customer usage.
  4. Offer proactive replacement units or dedicated technical support engineers instead of contract cancellations.

## SECTION 5: CAMPAIGN GUIDELINES & PROMOTIONS
- **Quarterly Push Campaigns**: Co-op marketing funds may be released up to 5% of monthly target revenue for regional events and partner enablement.
- **Bundling Incentives**: Approved bundles can include non-cash incentives such as 3 months of complimentary cloud telemetry.
- **Special Deal Registrations**: Protected margin locks are granted for registered enterprise deals held longer than 45 days.
